About Us

Who are we?

We’re Amani, Nour, and Natacha: three second-year Computer Science students who created this quiz platform as a simple way to test and improve our HTML, CSS, and JavaScript skills. What started as a tool for our own practice turned into something we wanted to share with anyone learning web development. The quizzes are designed to help you check what you know, spot weak points, and figure out which topics you should review or practice more; whether it’s styling, layout, or JavaScript fundamentals like the DOM and events. Our goal is to make learning more interactive, clear, and motivating, one question at a time.
